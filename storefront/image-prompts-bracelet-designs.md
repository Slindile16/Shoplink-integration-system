## Shared prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square luxury jewellery ecommerce product photograph. Subject: [subject below]. Product count and flower count are strict requirements. Entire product centered fully inside the frame, filling about 70 percent, clean warm pearl ivory matte background matching a refined jewellery catalogue. Photorealistic polished metal, enamel and cord textures, soft natural contact shadows and precise studio lighting. No text, no watermark, no logos, no props.
